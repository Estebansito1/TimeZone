AI Usage Notes

I used ChatGPT as a development assistant while building Time Zone in Unity.

AI was used to help with:
- Debugging Unity and C# errors
- Reviewing and improving gameplay scripts
- Explaining how Unity components, prefabs, colliders, UI elements, and references should be connected
- Building and troubleshooting the player movement and shooting systems
- Implementing asteroid damage, scoring, lives, health pickups, and level progression
- Implementing weapon upgrades from one shot to two shots and then a three-shot spread
- Implementing increasing asteroid speed and spawn frequency
- Implementing the scrolling background
- Adding level-up celebration ships and varying their movement
- Adding and troubleshooting sound effects
- Creating the Start Game and Game Over / Play Again systems
- Adding a persistent high-score system and “NEW HIGH SCORE” feedback
- Adding the flashing Play Again text
- Creating the hidden Up, Up, Down, Down support-squad feature
- Implementing support ships that move independently, fire at different rates, can be destroyed by asteroids, and contribute to the player's score
- Organizing and commenting the code for readability
- Troubleshooting Git setup and pushing the project to GitHub

I tested the game directly in Unity after each major change and adjusted the project based on the results. AI was used as a development and troubleshooting tool, while the final implementation was tested and verified in Unity.

I am prepared to explain the main scripts and gameplay systems used in the project, including PlayerController, Asteroid, AsteroidSpawner, HealthAsteroid, ScoreCounter, CelebrationShip, SupportShip, SupportCode, Laser, BackgroundScroll, StartMenu, and GameOverMenu.
